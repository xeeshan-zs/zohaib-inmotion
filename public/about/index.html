About Zohaib

I’m Zohaib, a media student and video editor exploring how editing, color, and sound turn a collection of shots into a story. I’m interested in the details: a pause, a perfectly timed cut, and the feeling a film leaves behind.

My core disciplines include edit direction, pacing, visual rhythm, color grading, and atmospheric sound beds. I believe that every frame should convey a specific feeling, and the true story is found in the cut. As a student, I am constantly learning and experimenting with different styles, from short films to social media edits and music videos.

When working on a project, I always start by finding the story. Then I build the rhythm, make it feel right with color and sound, and finally, polish the transitions to make the final cut as impactful as possible. This portfolio showcases my journey and the concept studies I've developed along the way.
