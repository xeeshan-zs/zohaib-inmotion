Zohaib — Video Editor Portfolio

I’m Zohaib, a media student and video editor exploring how editing, color, and sound turn a collection of shots into a story. I’m interested in the details: a pause, a perfectly timed cut, and the feeling a film leaves behind. I focus on edit direction, pacing, visual rhythm, color grading, and atmospheric sound beds.

Featured Concept Studies

  • Somewhere, Still. - A quiet road. An open horizon. A story told in the space between moments. This short film concept explores patient pacing, warm highlights, and restrained cuts across distance and quiet spaces.
  • After Hours - The city slows down. The details get louder. A social edit concept capturing urban night rhythm contrasting red streetlights with cool reflections, moving between wide establishing shots and rapid macro details.
  • Feel the Frequency - That split second when a room becomes a feeling. Visual performance energy focused on silhouettes, negative space, and dynamic cuts following the emotional contour of live audio.
